Banana Cue

Description

Banana cue is a popular Filipino street food made from bananas coated with caramelized brown sugar and deep-fried until golden brown.

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe saba bananas
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • Cooking oil
  • Barbecue sticks

Steps

  1. Peel the saba bananas.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan.
  3. Carefully add the bananas.
  4. Add brown sugar and cook until caramelized.
  5. Turn the bananas until coated with sugar.
  6. Remove carefully and let them cool.
  7. Insert barbecue sticks and serve.
