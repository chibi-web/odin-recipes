Banana Cue

Description

Banana Cue is a popular Filipino street food made from fried saba bananas coated with caramelized brown sugar. It is a sweet and delicious snack commonly served on bamboo skewers.

Ingredients

  • 6 pieces saba bananas
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 cups cooking oil
  • Bamboo skewers

Steps

  1. Peel the saba bananas.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan.
  3. Carefully add the bananas.
  4. Fry until they become slightly golden.
  5. Add brown sugar to the pan.
  6. Allow the sugar to melt and coat the bananas.
  7. Continue cooking until caramelized.
  8. Remove the bananas and let excess oil drain.
  9. Put the bananas on bamboo skewers.
  10. Serve and enjoy!
