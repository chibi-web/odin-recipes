Fried Egg

Description

Fried egg is a simple and delicious breakfast dish made by cooking an egg in a frying pan with oil.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat a frying pan over medium-low heat.
  2. Add cooking oil to the pan.
  3. Crack the egg into the pan.
  4. Cook until the egg white becomes firm.
  5. Add salt and pepper.
  6. Serve with rice or bread.
