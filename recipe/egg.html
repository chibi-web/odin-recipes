Fried Egg

Description

Fried egg is a simple and delicious dish commonly eaten for breakfast. It is made by cooking an egg in a frying pan with a little cooking oil.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • A pinch of salt
  • A pinch of black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat cooking oil in a frying pan.
  2. Crack the egg into the pan.
  3. Cook until the egg white is fully set.
  4. Add salt and black pepper.
  5. Carefully transfer the egg to a plate.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
