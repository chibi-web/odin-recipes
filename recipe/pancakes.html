Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are soft and fluffy breakfast food made from flour, milk, eggs, and other simple ingredients. They are easy to prepare and delicious when served with butter or syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. Mix flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until the ingredients are combined.
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  5. Lightly grease the pan with butter.
  6. Pour some pancake batter into the pan.
  7. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  8. Flip the pancake.
  9. Cook the other side until golden brown.
  10. Serve with butter or syrup. Enjoy!
