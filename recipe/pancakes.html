Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are soft and fluffy breakfast food made from flour, milk, eggs, and other simple ingredients.

Calories

Approximately 250 calories per serving.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Steps

  1. Mix flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. Add egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until combined.
  4. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour the pancake mixture into the pan.
  6. Cook until golden brown on both sides.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
